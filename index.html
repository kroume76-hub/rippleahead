RippleAhead turns El Niño forecasts into one simple answer for your neighborhood: what could happen, when, and what to do before it does.

01Risk for your blockA 0–100 risk score from 16-day rain and wind forecasts, local terrain and the El Niño signal.
02Countdown to the risk windowSee how many days you have, with a day-by-day prep checklist.
03Live creeks & alertsUSGS stream gauges near you and official National Weather Service alerts.
04Emergency kit31 essentials, with the items that matter most for your risk first.

Built on public data

Forecasts and observations come from NOAA Climate Prediction Center, the National Weather Service, USGS Water Services and Open-Meteo. Risk scores are estimates, not official forecasts. For evacuations and safety, always follow your local authorities.
