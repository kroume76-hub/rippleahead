Common questions

How is the risk score calculated?

For each of the next 16 days, RippleAhead adds up three-day rainfall compared with the amount that typically causes flooding in your area, how wet the ground already is, and how strongly El Niño is expected to boost rain this season. Strong wind gusts can raise the score on their own. The headline score is the highest day in the forecast. Tap the score in the app to see the breakdown.

The app doesn't show my location.

Go to Settings → Apps → RippleAhead → Location and choose "While Using the App". You can also pick an area from the menu at the top left without sharing your location.

How do I change the app's language?

Tap the globe button at the top of the My Area screen, or go to Settings → Apps → RippleAhead → Language. If the Language option is missing, first add a second language under Settings → General → Language & Region.

Why is the forecast "low confidence"?

Weather forecasts more than about a week out can change a lot. RippleAhead labels those days so you can plan without over-trusting them.

Is this an official warning service?

No. RippleAhead estimates risk from public data. For evacuations and safety, follow the National Weather Service and your local emergency officials.
